Our Story

Est. 2018

How It All Began

Recipe Secrets was born in a small Toa Payoh flat, where founder and head chef Marcus Koh would spend weekends recreating his late grandmother's recipes from memory. What started as a personal ritual soon became a shared obsession — friends would crowd around his tiny kitchen, asking for the recipes. His answer was always the same: "Every dish has a secret."

In 2018, Marcus turned that philosophy into a restaurant. Partnering with childhood friend and front-of-house director Claire Tan, they transformed a shophouse along Orchard Lane into a warm, inviting space that feels like coming home — even if you have never been before.

Today, Recipe Secrets is a beloved neighbourhood staple, recognised by the Singapore Tourism Board and featured in multiple local food guides. Yet the spirit remains the same: honest food, made with care, served with heart.

Head Chef

Marcus Koh

Founder & Executive Chef

Marcus trained at the At-Sunrice GlobalChef Academy before honing his skills at celebrated kitchens across Tokyo and Copenhagen. But it was his grandmother's kitchen that remained his greatest classroom — the place where he learned that the best flavours come from patience, intuition, and love.

His cooking philosophy centres on restraint: using fewer, better ingredients to let each element shine. He champions local produce, maintaining direct relationships with suppliers in Johor Bahru and Cameron Highlands to ensure every delivery is as fresh as possible.

When he is not in the kitchen, Marcus teaches community cooking classes for underprivileged youth through the non-profit he co-founded, The Shared Table Project.
